# SETU Social
The lack of a centralized platform for discovering social events may limit students' awareness of opportunities on and around the campus in the SETU community, which furthermore may negatively impacting their college experience. 

The proposed solution, An application designed as an all-in-one source that promotes social events and gatherings amongst Students and members of the SETU community to promote social engagement & interaction. 

 

The application will be developed as a cross-platform mobile application, allowing it to support both Android and iOS devices which is crucial for the application to run on our core userbase's varied devices.
